# Conformance waivers

Findings from `portolan check` that are accepted rather than fixed, with the
reasoning. Anything not listed here is a defect.

| Rule | Where | Why accepted | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|
| PTL-AST-003 (`file:size`/`file:checksum` missing) | `blocks/collection.json` asset `partitioned_data` | The asset's href is a hive glob (`./state_fips=*/*.parquet`) naming 52 files; a single size or checksum is undefined for a set. Each partition's own item asset carries both. | — |
| PTL-CAT-001 (52 children, no subcatalog) | `blocks/collection.json` | The children are the 52 hive partitions of one national dataset, keyed by state FIPS — a deliberate, machine-meaningful layout (`partition:*` fields + glob). Grouping them into subcatalogs would break the hive convention consumers rely on. | — |
| "1 registered asset missing from disk" notice | `blocks/collection.json` asset `partitioned_data` | The metadata scan treats the hive glob `./state_fips=*/*.parquet` (which `check --fix` itself wrote for PTL-PRT-001) as a literal path. Upstream portolan-cli bug; filed as [portolan-sdi/portolan-cli#1112](https://github.com/portolan-sdi/portolan-cli/issues/1112) (draft kept in [upstream-issue-portolan-cli-hive-items.md](upstream-issue-portolan-cli-hive-items.md)). Block items are named `NN.json` (data-file stem) as the workaround the scanner accepts. | [portolan-cli#1112](https://github.com/portolan-sdi/portolan-cli/issues/1112) |
| PTL-DAT-005 (declared bbox ⊂ data bbox, on both the GeoParquet and the `*-tiles` assets — the PMTiles additionally carry county and tract bands at coarse zooms, which reach past the collection's own framed footprint) | every collection | The spatial extent is a deliberate framing bbox: western hemisphere only, and for ACS collections only geographies that carry estimates. The data genuinely spans the antimeridian (Aleutians at +172°..+180°, Guam/CNMI at +144°) but a world-spanning or west>east bbox makes map clients open on the Atlantic — MapLibre fitBounds mishandles STAC antimeridian bboxes (filed as [portolan-sdi/portolan-browser#109](https://github.com/portolan-sdi/portolan-browser/issues/109); draft in [upstream-issue-portolan-browser-antimeridian.md](upstream-issue-portolan-browser-antimeridian.md)). The excluded geometries still ship in the data and are called out in each collection's known_issues. | [portolan-browser#109](https://github.com/portolan-sdi/portolan-browser/issues/109) |
| PTL-LIV-001/002/003/005 on host `www.census.gov` / `www2.census.gov` (~40 findings with `--live`) | every collection's `source-*`/`doc-*` assets | These probe the U.S. Census Bureau's own servers, which serve the linked upstream files without CORS headers, without Range support on dynamic pages, and without Content-Length on HTML. The catalog links the exact upstream files deliberately (mirror provenance); the catalog's own host, data.source.coop, is what consumers read data from. Not fixable from this repository. | — |
| PTL-DAT-017 (item lacks table:columns) | the 52 `blocks` partition items | Every partition shares the collection's schema; the 26 columns are documented once on `blocks/collection.json` (`table:columns`) rather than duplicated into 52 items and the items.parquet index. | — |
