Geography that carries no demographic statistic, so it stays out of the seven geography groups above. Four collections, all from TIGER/Line 2024 and the 2020 Census relationship files: [urban-areas](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/reference/urban-areas/collection.json) (2,644 features, the 2020 urban area delineation), [landmarks-area](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/reference/landmarks-area/collection.json) (120,734 civilian and military area landmarks), [landmarks-point](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/reference/landmarks-point/collection.json) (1,088,085 point landmarks) and [crosswalks](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/reference/crosswalks/collection.json) (22,429,855 rows across 29 tabular relationship tables, no geometry). None of the four shares a geometry or a `geoid` with another, so there is no 1:1 join across this group the way the ACS geographies join across their themed collections — each collection here stands on its own.
