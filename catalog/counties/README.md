American Community Survey 2020–2024 5-year indicators for all 3,235 counties and county equivalents in the United States, Puerto Rico and the island areas — the coarsest geography most questions are actually asked at, and the most reliable in this catalog below state level: the median coefficient of variation on median household income is 0.042 here against 0.125 at tract level (measured). Six collections share these rows. Five are the themed chunks the finer geographies carry — [income](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/counties/county-income/collection.json), [people](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/counties/county-people/collection.json), [housing](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/counties/county-housing/collection.json), [connectivity](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/counties/county-connectivity/collection.json) and [wellbeing](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/counties/county-wellbeing/collection.json), 23–34 columns each — and the sixth, [all indicators](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/counties/counties/collection.json), is the wide 112-column table, whose GeoParquet and PMTiles stay at `counties/counties.parquet` and `counties/counties.pmtiles`. All six hold the same `geoid` and the same geometry and join 1:1. One quirk to carry: `population_moe` is NULL on 3,103 of the 3,235 rows (measured). [data.census.gov](https://data.census.gov/) is authoritative.
