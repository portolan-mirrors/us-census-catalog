American Community Survey 2020–2024 5-year indicators for all 56 states and state equivalents — the 50 states, the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico and the four island areas, whose rows carry geometry and no ACS estimates. The 52 rows that do carry data hold the most reliable estimates in this catalog: no headline column has a median coefficient of variation above 0.037 (measured). Six collections share these rows — [income](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/states/state-income/collection.json), [people](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/states/state-people/collection.json), [housing](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/states/state-housing/collection.json), [connectivity](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/states/state-connectivity/collection.json), [wellbeing](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/states/state-wellbeing/collection.json) and the wide 112-column [all indicators](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/states/states/collection.json) table, whose GeoParquet and PMTiles stay at `states/states.parquet` and `states/states.pmtiles`. All six hold the same `geoid` and the same geometry and join 1:1; `population_moe` is NULL on all 56 rows (measured). [data.census.gov](https://data.census.gov/) is authoritative.
