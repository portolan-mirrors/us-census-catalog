American Community Survey 2020–2024 5-year indicators for all 32,612 incorporated places and [census designated places](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/bas/information/cdp.html) — every city, town, village, borough and named unincorporated community the Census Bureau delineates, from New York city to villages of 40 people. Places do not tile the country: the land between them is not in these files at all, so place rows never sum to a county or a state. Six collections share these rows: [income](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/places/place-income/collection.json), [people](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/places/place-people/collection.json), [housing](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/places/place-housing/collection.json), [connectivity](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/places/place-connectivity/collection.json), [wellbeing](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/places/place-wellbeing/collection.json) and the wide 112-column [all indicators](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/places/places/collection.json) table, whose GeoParquet and PMTiles stay at `places/places.parquet` and `places/places.pmtiles`. All six hold the same `geoid` and the same geometry and join 1:1. Most rows are small, so the suppression rates are high (`median_gross_rent` NULL on 31.32% of places, `median_hh_income` on 12.71%, measured): filter on `population` before reading a place-level distribution as if every row were a city. [data.census.gov](https://data.census.gov/) is authoritative.
