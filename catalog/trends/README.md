A dozen headline indicators across two non-overlapping ACS 5-year releases, 2015-2019 and 2020-2024, long form — one row per `geoid` per `period`. Three collections, one per geography: [county-trends](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/trends/county-trends/collection.json) (3,235 counties), [state-trends](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/trends/state-trends/collection.json) (56 states) and [tract-trends](https://source.coop/portolan-mirrors/us-census-catalog/trends/tract-trends/collection.json) (85,184 tracts). Every row carries `geo_change` — `identical`, `absorbed`, `subdivided`, `mixed` or `no_acs_data` — measured per geoid against the real 2010-to-2020 tract crosswalk and the 2022 Connecticut county realignment, never assumed, and the period a geoid's lineage cannot legitimately support carries NULL rather than an apportioned number. The `chg_*` columns carry the 2020-2024-minus-2015-2019 difference, already computed, on the 2020-2024 row. These three collections carry only 12 of the catalog's ~135 indicators; for everything else, or for a 2020-2024-only question, see the counties, states and tracts groups.
